# CCDV-F Mock Exam Question Bank — Sources & Provenance

This platform synthesizes **636 authentic scenario-based developer questions** structured into **12 full-length 53-question practice exams** for the **Anthropic Claude Certified Developer – Foundations (CCDV-F)** certification.

Every question has been vetted strictly for **Developer (CCDV-F)** scope, aligned to the official 8-domain exam blueprint, and provided with full technical explanations and distractor analyses.

> [!NOTE]
> **Hard Tier Exams (#11 & #12) — Authored from Scratch:**
> Exams **#11** and **#12** are slightly harder than the rest of the exams and were generated completely from scratch directly against the official Anthropic CCDV-F blueprint specifications. Unlike Exams 1–10 (which synthesize community practice banks), Exams 11 and 12 feature elevated distractor plausibility and edge-case testing, and are **not part of other mocks available across sources or community dumps**.

---

## Educational Purpose & Anti-Plagiarism Statement

The objective of this exam simulator is **educational synthesis and fair practice, not plagiarism**. 

Single-source question banks often suffer from narrow authorial bias, idiosyncratic phrasing, and blind spots. By aggregating, vetting, and balancing high-quality questions across multiple independent community creators and scratch-authored hard forms matching Anthropic's official blueprint, candidates gain realistic, unbiased preparation for the actual proctored exam.

- **Non-Commercial / Purely Educational:** This simulator is 100% free and open for developer community preparation.
- **Strict Attribution:** All questions preserve author and repository provenance in `data/sources_metadata.json` and are viewable in the in-app "Sources & Provenance" view.
- **No Exam Dumps:** No proprietary Pearson VUE exam disclosures or non-public NDA materials are used. All questions originate from community-authored open-source study repositories or original blueprint-aligned hard scenarios.
